Clues from everyday life

Why do coleoptiles（胚芽鞘） near a window bend towards light?

This experiment uses oat coleoptiles（胚芽鞘）. A coleoptile（胚芽鞘） is a sheath that covers and protects the young leaves inside. Its fairly straight shape makes changes easy to observe.
