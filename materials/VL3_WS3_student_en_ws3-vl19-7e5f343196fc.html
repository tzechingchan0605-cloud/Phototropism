Topic notes

Phototropism（向光性） is growth in response to light direction; growth towards light is positive phototropism（向光性）. A coleoptile（胚芽鞘） protects young leaves.

The tip senses light. Its growth-promoting effect passes down. Auxin（生長素） is a plant hormone, not light or a nutrient.

Coleoptile（胚芽鞘）: more auxin（生長素） on the shaded rightCell detail: more elongation（延長） on the right

Light on the left → shaded right side → more auxin（生長素） passes down → right-side cells elongate（延長） more → bending left.

Agar（瓊脂） supports a transferable growth-promoting effect, not its identity or distribution under light.
