04 · Extension 2

Can substance X transfer a growth-promoting effect? How does its position affect bending?

Researchers have prepared agar（瓊脂） that has been in contact with coleoptile（胚芽鞘） tips to test whether this effect can be transferred and how its position affects bending.

Extension 2 materials: oat coleoptiles（胚芽鞘） with tips removed × 4, growing containers × 4, blank agar（瓊脂） × 1, treated agar（瓊脂） × 3, timer × 1.

Both types of agar（瓊脂） look the same. Blank agar（瓊脂） has not been in contact with coleoptile（胚芽鞘） tips; Treated agar（瓊脂） has been in contact with coleoptile（胚芽鞘） tips. Some substances can enter and pass through agar（瓊脂）.

Four extension 2 setups in darkness

All coleoptiles（胚芽鞘） have their tips removed, start at a similar size and are kept in darkness. Agar（瓊脂） size, growing time, temperature and water supply are the same. You can view them before and after growth. Placing agar（瓊脂） on a coleoptile（胚芽鞘） does not physically hold it down.

Growing time, gradual growth animations and angles are teaching simulations, not real measurements or exact experimental constants.
