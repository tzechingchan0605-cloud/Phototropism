3. Which part may sense light from one side? Tip

1. Compare A and B: What changes when light is blocked at the tip? No clear bending towards the light.

2. Compare A and C: Does it still bend when the lower part receives no direct light? It can still bend towards light.

1. What does comparing elongation（延長） in A and D support? The presence of the tip also affects the coleoptile（胚芽鞘）’s growth / elongation（延長）.

1. What does the comparison of E and F support? The tip may produce a growth-promoting effect that can be transferred.

2. What does the comparison of F, G and H support? The position of treated agar（瓊脂） affects the direction of coleoptile（胚芽鞘） bending.

3. When treated agar（瓊脂） is placed on the left side of a coleoptile（胚芽鞘） with its tip removed, compared with the right side, the left side receives more substance X, causing cells on the left to elongate（延長） more and the coleoptile（胚芽鞘） to bend to the right side.

A–D: whether the tip is present. E–F: whether agar（瓊脂） previously touched the tip, with both in the centre. F–G–H: treated agar（瓊脂） position, with the same agar（瓊脂） type. The main comparison changes shading treatment.

Reflection and correction

Right-side light makes the left the shaded side. The tip senses light; more auxin（生長素） on the left promotes greater left-cell elongation（延長）, so the coleoptile（胚芽鞘） bends towards the light on the right.

C2 correction: retain first answers, identify a correction and one data-based reason. If initially correct, provide supporting evidence. The causal chain must show greater elongation（延長） on the shaded side, not the lit side.

Cutting creates a wound and removes tissue, so it cannot establish a unique mechanism alone. Dark agar（瓊脂） results support a transferable growth-promoting effect and unequal elongation（延長）. Bending is not caused by light during incubation; the results neither measure auxin（生長素） concentration directly nor identify the substance.
