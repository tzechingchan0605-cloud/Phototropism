Why do coleoptiles（胚芽鞘） near a window bend towards light?

This experiment uses oat coleoptiles（胚芽鞘）. A coleoptile（胚芽鞘） is a sheath that covers and protects the young leaves inside. Its fairly straight shape makes changes easy to observe.

04 · Extension 2

Can substance X transfer a growth-promoting effect? How does its position affect bending?

Researchers have prepared agar（瓊脂） that has been in contact with coleoptile（胚芽鞘） tips to test whether this effect can be transferred and how its position affects bending.

Extension 2 materials: oat coleoptiles（胚芽鞘） with tips removed × 4, growing containers × 4, blank agar（瓊脂） × 1, treated agar（瓊脂） × 3, timer × 1.

Both types of agar（瓊脂） look the same. Blank agar（瓊脂） has not been in contact with coleoptile（胚芽鞘） tips; Treated agar（瓊脂） has been in contact with coleoptile（胚芽鞘） tips. Some substances can enter and pass through agar（瓊脂）.

Four extension 2 setups in darkness

All coleoptiles（胚芽鞘） have their tips removed, start at a similar size and are kept in darkness. Agar（瓊脂） size, growing time, temperature and water supply are the same. You can view them before and after growth. Placing agar（瓊脂） on a coleoptile（胚芽鞘） does not physically hold it down.

Growing time, gradual growth animations and angles are teaching simulations, not real measurements or exact experimental constants.

From evidence to explanation

How plants grow towards light

  1. Directional plant growth in response to light direction is called "phototropism（向光性）"; growth towards light is called "positive phototropism（向光性）".
  2. In this oat coleoptile（胚芽鞘） model, Tip helps sense light from one side.
  3. Other research helps us understand the substance called "substance X" in this inquiry as Auxin（生長素）. Auxin（生長素） is a hormone that affects plant growth; the tip can produce a growth-promoting effect that passes downwards.
  4. With light from one side, more auxin（生長素） is found on the shaded side, so cells on that side elongate（延長） more, making the coleoptile（胚芽鞘） bend towards light.

Learning reflection

The light source is on the right, and the coleoptile（胚芽鞘） tip senses light from one side. Auxin（生長素） moves towards the left (shaded side) and passes downwards, causing cells on the left to elongate（延長） more. The coleoptile（胚芽鞘） therefore bends towards the light on the right, showing positive phototropism（向光性）. The teacher reviews this; it is not automatically marked wrong.
