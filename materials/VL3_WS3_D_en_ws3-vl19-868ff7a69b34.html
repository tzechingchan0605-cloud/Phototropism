Reference answers and acceptable wording

Variables: IV shading; DV coleoptile（胚芽鞘） bending; CV light direction/intensity, species/initial height/stage, duration, temperature and water. Assumptions: similar initial condition, unrestricted growth and no marked temperature difference; reject species alone removes size control. Control: intact uncovered A under the same other conditions.

Shading answers in order: no clear bending towards light; can still bend towards light; tip. Tip removal: presence of the tip also affects growth/elongation（延長）. Agar（瓊脂）: the tip may produce a transferable growth-promoting effect; placement affects bending direction; more, more, right. See the complete answer worksheet for every question, option, figure and answer in its original position.

Integrated explanation: one point per causal link

1. The tip senses light from the right.

2. The left side is shaded and has more auxin（生長素）.

3. The growth-promoting effect passes down from the tip.

4. Consequently, cells on the left elongate（延長） more than those on the right.

5. Unequal elongation（延長） causes bending towards the light on the right.

One point per correct causal link, total 5. Accept scientifically sound equivalent wording; do not count keywords or award the same link twice. Missing links receive no point; assessed unrelated responses score 0; unassessed responses remain blank.

Biology | Phototropic growth in plants

Scientific limits and teaching objectives

Shading supports tip involvement in sensing light. Cutting also wounds and removes other tissues, so cannot alone establish the sensing mechanism. No elongation（延長） in D is a model setting, not a universal rule for cut plants. E/F supports a transferable growth-promoting effect without identifying auxin（生長素） or its distribution under unilateral light. In darkness, left-placed treated agar（瓊脂） promotes left-side elongation（延長） and bending right, with the reverse for right placement. Central treated agar（瓊脂） produces straight elongation（延長）; central blank agar（瓊脂） gives no clear elongation（延長）.

Objectives: identify variables and controls, read qualitative outcomes and angles, infer sensing and growth effects from the three comparisons, and explain bending causally. Static reading is not dynamic operation; model values are not measurements. Do not restore deleted graphs, prescribed comparisons or extra follow-ups. Check derived answers against the student's own valid readings without cascading penalties; this WS adds no derived question.
