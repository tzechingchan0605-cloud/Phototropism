3. When treated agar（瓊脂） is placed on the left side of a coleoptile（胚芽鞘） with its tip removed, compared with the right side, the left side receives more substance X, causing cells on the left to elongate（延長） more and the coleoptile（胚芽鞘） to bend to the right side.

Learning reflection

The light source is on the right, and the coleoptile（胚芽鞘） tip senses light from one side. Auxin（生長素） moves towards the left (shaded side) and passes downwards, causing cells on the left to elongate（延長） more. The coleoptile（胚芽鞘） therefore bends towards the light on the right, showing positive phototropism（向光性）. The teacher reviews this; it is not automatically marked wrong.
