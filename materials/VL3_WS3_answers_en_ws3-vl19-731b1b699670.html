Topic notes

  • Phototropism（向光性） is growth in response to light direction. Shoots usually grow towards light (positive phototropism（向光性）). The left diagram shows a bending coleoptile（胚芽鞘）: part of the seedling’s shoot that protects young leaves.
  • The tip senses light from one side and contains auxin（生長素）, a chemical substance that is a plant hormone. More auxin（生長素） passes down the shaded side, stimulating cells in the elongation（延長） region to elongate（延長） and promote growth.
  • Agar（瓊脂） allows chemical substances to pass through it.

Part 3 | Integrated explanation

14.Use the experimental results and topic notes to explain why the coleoptile（胚芽鞘） in the diagram bends to the right.

Light is on the right and sensed at the tip. More auxin（生長素） is on the shaded left side and passes down. Left-side cells elongate（延長） more, causing bending towards the light on the right (positive phototropism（向光性）).
